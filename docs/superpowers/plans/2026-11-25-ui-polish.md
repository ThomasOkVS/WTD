## Findings (ranked by demo impact)

1. **Pests are hard to see.** Flies and corkscrews are small and dark on the dark path. On a projector they vanish. *Best practice: enemies must be the highest-contrast thing on the field.* (Phase B)
2. **No button hierarchy.** Every button is the same burgundy: Resume and Back to title, Upgrade and Sell, Keep pouring and Claim victory. *Best practice: one primary action per dialog, secondary actions quieter, destructive actions distinct.*
3. **Modal titles are low-contrast.** Gold "Paused", "The cellar holds!" and "Controls" are set on cream parchment (`serif(…, PALETTE.candleGold)`).
4. **The upgrade panel doesn't say what the upgrade does.** It shows "Next: Senior Recruiter" with no effect. It also has no close affordance, and Sell looks like Upgrade. (Phase B)
5. **No onboarding cue on an empty cellar.** Nothing says "pick a tower card, click the floor", and nothing shows where pests enter. *Best practice: the first 10 seconds teach the core verb.*
6. **The wave banner overlaps the Wine Tasting title.** "Wave 3 cleared" draws over the Tasting heading. (Phase B)
7. **No boss health bar.** The Le Monstre Blanc fight has no HUD bar, only a small bar over its sprite. *Best practice: bosses get a big screen-space bar.* (Phase B)
8. **The End screens are thin.**
   - No Back to title.
   - Stats are only waves and kills: no lives left, and no Endless wave line if missing.
   - The defeat screen has no art. (The win screen has the bottle.)
9. **The Tasting screen:**
   - All three wine cards use the identical bottle.
   - Thomas's speech bubble breaks awkwardly ("I would hire / it.").
10. **The title screen:**
    - It says "seven waves", but Endless exists now.
    - No hint of the controls.
    - The Grand Cru bottle is cut off at the right edge.
